# DAGIT published service notices

**Public URL:** `https://dagit.macula.co.za/legal`
**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Support:** support@macula.co.za

The public service-notices page is the concise, customer-facing statement of DAGIT's operating boundaries. It is backed by these detailed product documents:

- `TERMS_OF_USE_DRAFT.md` — published service terms and product boundaries;
- `PRIVACY_NOTICE_DRAFT.md` — published privacy/data notice, including retention/deletion and provider categories;
- `SECURITY_AND_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md` — published incident process and support boundary; and
- `FIRM_DATA_HANDLING_AND_RETENTION_STANDARD.md` — the additional operational baseline for a firm using the protected firm workflow.

The service notices do not claim that DAGIT is a document repository, electronic-signature provider, identity service, wallet custodian, legal adviser, or proof of ownership/authority/truth. They do not override a firm's own client, legal-hold, records, signing or professional obligations.

For a wallet-owned public workspace, DAGIT keeps only the organisation name, wallet address, workspace alias, anchored receipt metadata and necessary operational audit data while the workspace remains active. A workspace owner may request deletion of that off-chain metadata through support@macula.co.za. Files are not uploaded, and public Chain 1404 records cannot generally be removed.
