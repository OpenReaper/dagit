# DAGIT legal and privacy information

**Public URL:** `https://dagit.macula.co.za/legal`
**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Support:** support@macula.co.za

The public legal-and-privacy page explains the information people need when using DAGIT:

- [Terms of use](TERMS_OF_USE.md);
- [Privacy notice](PRIVACY_NOTICE.md); and
- support and incident guidance at support@macula.co.za.

The service notices do not claim that DAGIT is a document repository, electronic-signature provider, identity service, wallet custodian, legal adviser, or proof of ownership/authority/truth. They do not override a firm's own client, legal-hold, records, signing or professional obligations.

Files are not uploaded. A workspace keeps the details needed to find its saved proofs while it remains active. A workspace owner may ask to remove that off-chain workspace information through support@macula.co.za. Public Chain 1404 records cannot generally be removed.
