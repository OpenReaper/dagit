# DAGIT terms of use

**Effective date:** 5 October 2026
**Support:** support@macula.co.za

These terms explain how to use DAGIT — Digital Asset Guarantee & Integrity Tool. DAGIT is presented by Macula. A DAGIT proof does not replace a legal signature, identity check, ownership record, or professional advice.

## 1. Service

DAGIT — Digital Asset Guarantee & Integrity Tool — provides a technical file-integrity service. It can create a local cryptographic fingerprint, enable a wallet owner to register a proof commitment on BlockDAG Chain 1404, produce a portable receipt, and compare a selected local file with that receipt.

The service is not a document repository, identity-verification service, electronic-signature provider, law firm, legal adviser, escrow, wallet custodian, payment service, or blockchain validator.

## 2. What a proof means

A proof is evidence that a supplied fingerprint was recorded by a wallet in an identified blockchain transaction and that a selected file matched that fingerprint at the time of local verification. It does not establish identity, authority, authorship, ownership, delivery, receipt, consent, intent, legal validity, enforceability, truth, completeness, or the absence of fraud.

## 3. User responsibilities

Users must:

- use the service only for lawful purposes and with the required rights and authority;
- protect wallets, recovery material, account credentials, devices, and proof receipts;
- review every wallet prompt, network and transaction fee before approval;
- keep original records and signing-provider evidence in their own systems;
- not put confidential content, personal data, credentials, malware, unlawful material, or misleading assertions in public or shared proof fields; and
- use a qualified signing, identity, legal, or records professional where their workflow requires one.

## 4. Fees, wallets and blockchain operation

Users choose whether to submit an anchor transaction. The user’s wallet asks for the applicable BDAG network fee before submission. Blockchain transaction fees, confirmation timing, network availability, and third-party wallet operation are outside the provider’s control. An approved on-chain transaction is generally irreversible.

The provider does not hold private keys, recover wallets, reverse blockchain transactions, or guarantee that a public blockchain record will remain available forever.

## 5. Prohibited use

Users must not use DAGIT to:

- misrepresent a proof as legal execution, identity verification, ownership, or proof of truth;
- bypass access controls, probe other users’ records, or interfere with the service;
- upload or expose another person’s confidential information without authority;
- introduce malware or attempt to compromise wallets, accounts, networks, or systems; or
- use the service in any manner that violates applicable law, professional obligations, sanctions, or third-party rights.

## 6. Firm workspace

Where a firm workspace is provided, the firm is responsible for authorising staff, protecting its identity-provider accounts, setting matter access, configuring retention, and using its own signing and case-management systems. The firm must not treat the workspace as the authoritative document, case, identity, or signature record.

## 7. Service changes and availability

The provider may change, suspend, or retire features where reasonably necessary for security, maintenance, legal compliance, or product operation. The service depends on browsers, wallet software, hosting providers, identity providers, and blockchain infrastructure. Availability and verification results are not guaranteed.

## 8. Service limits and availability

DAGIT is provided as a technical integrity tool. It depends on the user’s browser, device, wallet, internet connection, Chain 1404 nodes, hosting and identity providers. A failure to obtain a chain result is not a match. Users must keep their own document, signing, identity and evidence records.

## 9. Support and incident contact

For product support, privacy questions, a request concerning off-chain workspace metadata, or a suspected product-security incident, contact [support@macula.co.za](mailto:support@macula.co.za). DAGIT support cannot retrieve a file, recover a wallet, reverse a blockchain transaction, access a signing-provider account, or give legal advice.
